What is Atlantic™?

An AI assistant made by GSC Studios.

You talk to it the way you talk to a person: ask it to write an email, explain a lesson, work out a calculation, throw ideas around or generate an image. Everything happens in the browser — open the link, sign in with your GSCNET account and type your first message.

It is part of the GSC ecosystem, alongside GSCNET — your private file vault and temporary link sharing. One account, the same data, the same experience.
