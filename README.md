## What is Atlantic™?

Atlantic™ is an AI assistant built by **GSC Studios**. You talk to it the way you talk to a person: ask it to write an email, explain a lesson, work out a calculation, throw ideas around or generate an image. Everything happens in the browser — no install, no setup, nothing to configure.

Open the link, sign in with your GSCNET account and type your first message.

---

## What you can do with it

- **Natural conversation** — questions, explanations, summaries, rewrites and ideas on any topic.
- **Image generation** — describe what you want to see and get the image, right in the conversation.
- **Sites & Projects** — keep your work organised in projects, each with its own space.
- **Chat search** — find the conversation from three days ago in an instant.
- **Attachments, images and voice** — hand it a file, a photo, or just talk; no copy-pasting.
- **Several models in one place** — Atl0s Mini, Atl0s, Atl0s Vision and Atl0s Pro; pick from the menu depending on what you need.

## Getting started

1. Open **[gscnet.go.ro/atlantic](https://gscnet.go.ro/atlantic)**.
2. Sign in with your **GSCNET** account — the same account across the whole GSC ecosystem.
3. Type whatever is on your mind. Atlantic™ takes it from there.

There is a **FREE** plan to try it out, and you can upgrade any time with a click. Your chats and projects stay yours, whatever the plan.
